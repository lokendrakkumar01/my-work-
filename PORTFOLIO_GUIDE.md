## 5. 🎯 5 Core Features to Demo in an Interview

Jab interview me aapse is project ke baare me pucha jaye, toh in 5 features ka demo dekar samjhayein:

1. **YouTube 7-Stage Pipeline (`/youtube`)**:
   - Samjhayein ki kaise video idea se script, recording, editing, optimizing, publishing tak ek-ek stage me advance hota hai.
2. **Multi-Channel Social Hub (`/social`)**:
   - Twitter (280 char limit validation), LinkedIn, aur Instagram ke liye separate scheduling aur post drafting.
3. **Interactive Task & Kanban Manager (`/tasks`)**:
   - 1-Click status toggle (`todo` ➜ `in-progress` ➜ `completed`), priority filters (urgent, high, medium, low), and instant search.
4. **Gemini AI Copilot (`/ai`)**:
   - Real-time Gemini 1.5 Flash generation for captions, full YouTube video scripts, viral hashtags, and productivity sprint roadmaps.
5. **Performance & Productivity Analytics (`/analytics`)**:
   - High-level metric calculations, task sprint velocity percentages, and channel distribution breakdown meters.
